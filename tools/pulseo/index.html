PulseO

Your Search Console data in your own Google Sheets, every week, with reports that tell you what changed and what needs attention. I built PulseO for my own client work and made it open source.

View on GitHub Privacy policy
Features

What PulseO does

Weekly sync

Queries, pages, devices, countries and dates from Search Console, written to Google Sheets on a schedule.

History that does not expire

Search Console keeps about 16 months. PulseO keeps every finished day for as long as you keep the sheet.

Reports and alarms

Monthly and weekly report tabs, growing and declining keywords and pages, year over year comparisons, and alarms when clicks drop.

Decks by email

Weekly and monthly PowerPoint decks sent to the people you choose.

Process

How it works

  1. You connect PulseO to your Search Console property with read-only access.
  2. It runs on a schedule in your own GitHub account and writes to Google Sheets you choose.
  3. You read the reports, or let the decks land in your inbox.
Privacy

Your data stays yours

  • Read-only access to Search Console. PulseO never changes anything in your account.
  • It writes only to the spreadsheets you set up for it.
  • There is no PulseO server or database. Data moves from Google to your own Google Sheets.
  • No ads, no tracking of your data, and nothing is sold.
  • You can remove access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

PulseO's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
